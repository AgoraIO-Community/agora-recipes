# Agora Recipes

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

RTE conference visitors arriving by QR code need to understand what they can build and find an existing demo. Returning developers need to find a specific capability and its implementation guide.

## Product Purpose

A searchable catalog of Agora voice AI and real-time communication recipes. Recipe pages combine implementation metadata with markdown fetched from the implementation repository at build time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The local catalog includes every recipe. Six existing demo recipes are recommended first, with compact thumbnails and actions. More entries peek through a natural inline expansion; search and filters always cover every recipe. Video plays on-site and live demos open externally. There is no event-specific collection or View all destination. No new demo services or deployment are in scope. Catalog and curation remain in the repository; database, upload infrastructure, and expanded contribution flows are deferred.

Dark mode is the first-visit default with a persistent theme switch. Local search includes descriptions and card summaries, tolerates small typos, and recognizes common English/Chinese domain terms. It does not require a hosted search service.

## Brand Commitments

Use the current Agora Website 2026 visual family and selected original agent-ui Glass components supplied by the user. Keep real product media, clear outcomes, compact mobile browsing, and simple interactions. Existing catalog copy is English.

## Evidence on Hand

The approved six recipes are Agora Rotary Dial Phone, Agora Podcasts, Aria Voice Sales Agent, Agora Meeting Copilot, WorldCupVoice, and React Live Video. They have existing screenshots, video, or GIF assets. Meeting Copilot requires an API key for its AI experience. WorldCupVoice has an existing video but no verified public live demo. Public entry pages and video playback were checked; complete voice and multi-device RTC sessions were not verified.

## Product Principles

- Explain the outcome before implementation details.
- Let visitors see or try existing work before reading a recipe.
- Keep media availability and setup requirements truthful.
- Keep curation lightweight and editable in the repository.
